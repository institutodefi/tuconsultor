ISO 21001

How long does it take to implement ISO 21001?+
It depends on your organisation's size and starting point. In the initial diagnosis we give you a plan with realistic deadlines; most projects are completed in a few months of agile, continuous work.

Just a first conversation. Write to hola@tuconsultor.com or book a meeting: we understand your situation, answer questions and propose the right service level, with no commitment.
