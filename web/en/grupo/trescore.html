Purpose
Our purpose
TRESCORE PROYECTOS ITE, S.L. is the group's parent company and operates under the TuConsultor brand. Its purpose is to transform the concept of consulting through a client-relationship model that enables Consulting as a Service: the Digital, Human, Cutting-edge Ecosystem. A team of consultants always available digitally with integrated tools, structured in three dimensions — documentation, technology and talent — and four service levels: Relación, Implicación, Compromiso and Apoyo.
∞
Vision
To be the first consulting firm that integrates organisations into its ecosystem.
What it does

Two lines of work.

01

Management systems consulting

Implementation, maintenance and audit preparation for ISO systems, the Spanish National Security Framework (ENS) and models of excellence and sustainability. Nine areas of expertise and four service levels —Relación, Implicación, Compromiso and Apoyo— so each organisation chooses the intensity of support.

02

Special corporate projects

Assignments that do not fit a standard management system: bespoke projects defined with the management team, with their own scope, team and deadlines, handled with the same documentary and technological methodology as the rest of the services.
